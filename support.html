Subscription Help

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. To view or cancel your subscription, open iPhone Settings, tap your Apple ID name, then tap Subscriptions and select Morph: Breath & Focus.

Refunds and cancellation handling are subject to Apple policies and the billing method used for your purchase.

Restore Purchases

If you purchased premium access but it is not active in the app, open Morph: Breath & Focus and use the Restore Purchases option from the paywall or settings.
