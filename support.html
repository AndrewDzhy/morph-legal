Subscription Help

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. You can manage or cancel your subscription from your iPhone settings:

  • Open iPhone Settings.
  • Tap your Apple ID name.
  • Tap Subscriptions.
  • Select Morph: Breath & Focus.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription.

Restore Purchases

If you purchased premium access but it is not active in the app, open the app and use the Restore Purchases option on the paywall or in settings.
