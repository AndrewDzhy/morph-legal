Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Morph: Breath & Focus collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the app and related web pages.

Last updated: 27.05.2026

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • profile information you choose to enter in the app, such as a name or nickname;
  • settings and preferences, such as reminders, app settings, and feature toggles;
  • usage data, such as screen views, taps, session starts and completions, sound selections, and mood interactions;
  • subscription data, such as trial status, purchase status, receipts, and entitlement state;
  • content you choose to store locally, such as mood entries, session history, onboarding responses, and personal preferences;
  • technical data, such as device type, iOS version, IP address, diagnostic logs, crash data, and analytics identifiers where available and allowed.

2. No Account Required

Morph: Breath & Focus does not require you to create an account or sign in to use the core app experience.

We do not maintain a user account database and we do not use Supabase or a similar account-sync service in the app.

3. Information Stored Locally

Morph: Breath & Focus uses a local-first design. This means that some information is stored directly on your device, including:

  • your name or nickname, if you choose to enter one;
  • your preferences and app settings;
  • breathing session history;
  • mood entries;
  • your last selected sound;
  • onboarding state;
  • cached subscription state, where supported.

Local data may be protected by the security features of your device and operating system.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party providers to help run Morph: Breath & Focus. These providers may process certain information in accordance with their own terms and policies.

  • Apple / App Store - for in-app purchases, subscriptions, and receipt handling.
  • RevenueCat - for subscription management, receipts, and entitlement state.
  • PostHog - for product analytics and usage insights.

We do not use Supabase in the app.

5. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • provide and maintain the app;
  • personalize the experience;
  • save your progress, preferences, and history;
  • process subscriptions, trials, and purchase restoration;
  • measure product usage, improve UX, and understand retention;
  • diagnose bugs, crashes, and technical issues;
  • send service-related messages where applicable.

6. Do We Sell Your Information?

No. We do not sell your personal information. We also do not share it with third parties for their own advertising purposes.

7. Analytics and Tracking

We may collect product events such as screen views, session starts, sound selections, paywall views, trial starts, purchases, and mood logging events.

We use these data points to:

  • improve onboarding and product flow;
  • measure retention and conversion;
  • understand which features are used;
  • identify product issues and opportunities.

We do not use analytics data for third-party advertising.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law.

Because Morph: Breath & Focus does not create user accounts, there is no in-app account deletion flow. You can remove locally stored data by deleting the app from your device, and you may contact us if you need help understanding what data remains with third-party providers such as Apple or RevenueCat.

9. Children

Morph: Breath & Focus is not intended for children under 13 years old. If you believe we have collected information from a child without the appropriate legal basis, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

10. International Data Transfers

If you use the service from outside the country where our providers are located, your information may be processed in other countries. In such cases, we attempt to apply appropriate safeguards as required by law.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If the changes are material, we may notify you in the app or update the date on this page. Continued use of the service after changes become effective means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

If you have questions about privacy, data deletion, or this policy, contact us at morphbreath@icloud.com or use the support contact listed in the app.

This Privacy Policy applies to Morph: Breath & Focus and any related pages that link to it.
