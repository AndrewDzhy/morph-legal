Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Morph: Breath & Focus ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app.

1. Information We May Collect

Morph: Breath & Focus may collect limited information needed to provide app features, improve the product, manage subscriptions, and support user accounts.

Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • App usage events, such as screen views, feature usage, and interaction events.
  • Subscription and purchase status.
  • Account-related information if sign-in features are enabled.
  • Device and technical information such as app version, device type, and operating system version.
  • Support messages you send to us by email.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Provide and improve app functionality.
  • Manage subscriptions and premium access.
  • Understand how users interact with the app.
  • Fix bugs and improve performance.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Maintain app safety and reliability.

3. Third-Party Services

Morph: Breath & Focus may use third-party services to operate and improve the app. These services may process limited data according to their own privacy policies.

These services may include:

  • RevenueCat for subscription management and purchase status.
  • PostHog for product analytics and app usage insights.
  • Supabase for authentication, account, or backend-related features.
  • Apple services for App Store purchases, subscriptions, and account management.

4. Subscriptions and Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not directly collect or store your full payment card information.

5. Analytics

We may use analytics to understand general app usage, improve onboarding, measure feature performance, and make the app better. Analytics data is used for product improvement and is not sold to advertisers.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited information with service providers only when necessary to operate, analyze, or improve the app.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as needed to provide the app, support users, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and improve the product.

8. Children’s Privacy

Morph: Breath & Focus is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under the age required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

9. Health and Wellness Disclaimer

Morph: Breath & Focus is designed for general wellness, relaxation, focus, and sleep support. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

10. Your Choices

You may manage subscriptions through your Apple ID settings. You may also contact us to ask questions about privacy or support-related issues.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: morphbreath@icloud.com
